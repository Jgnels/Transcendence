# Gate 0 — Native CAI Diagnostic Telemetry v0.2M

## Gate decision

`PLAYER_VISIBLE_CHURN_INSTRUMENT_NOT_QUALIFIED -> PRIVILEGED_RESEARCH_TELEMETRY_PREPARED`

The v0.2L vanilla run produced zero eligible windows because all 187 candidate windows were eliminated by visibility censoring or stationary/non-directional movement. Under D109, the SFO v0.2L match is skipped.

## What v0.2M may do

- build a deterministic read-only diagnostic pack;
- observe complete non-human faction forces at their own turn start/end;
- record regions/wars needed for later offline context reconstruction;
- qualify telemetry density using preregistered exposure thresholds;
- package research evidence for offline analysis.

## What v0.2M may not do

- issue campaign or battle orders;
- mutate saves or campaign state;
- alter DB rows;
- feed privileged hidden state into a future gameplay/application controller;
- call a descriptive movement reversal native task churn;
- revive v0.2G or v0.2I.

## Live exit criteria

The first vanilla diagnostic capture either:

1. meets all four frozen exposure thresholds and earns a **new offline preregistration step**, or
2. fails one or more thresholds and triggers another instrumentation redesign.

It cannot itself earn a strategic planner or a CAI tuning change.
