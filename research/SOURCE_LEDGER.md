# Source Ledger

Import the prior 25-project audit and machine-readable ledger into this repository, preserving original hashes.

For every new source, record:

- canonical URL;
- exact revision or artifact date;
- license;
- files inspected;
- architecture findings;
- acquisition classification;
- copied/adapted files;
- local modifications;
- tests;
- notices.

The six owner-provided AI Workshop examples and SFO are benchmark/reference artifacts unless legal source access and reuse permission are separately established.
