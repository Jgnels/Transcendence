# Project Charter

## Product definition

This is initially a private, personal enhancement project rather than a Workshop release.

It may improve:

- campaign strategic AI;
- operational army coordination;
- battle behavior where technically controllable;
- late-game challenge and rival power formation;
- diplomacy and empire management;
- pacing, meaningful threats and campaign continuity;
- observability and explanation;
- SFO compatibility and personal configuration.

It is not limited to “AI” when another bounded game-system change better serves the owner's desired experience.

## North star

Create campaigns in which the owner can become powerful without the world becoming inert.

The player should still face coherent rival states, meaningful fronts, dangerous offensives, hard battlefield choices and plausible recovery by opponents. Difficulty should come primarily from coordination, strategic pressure, asymmetric faction strengths and well-formed threats—not arbitrary omniscience or invisible stat inflation.

## Initial optimization target

- Total War: WARHAMMER III
- SFO: Grimhammer III
- single player
- hardest campaign and battle difficulty
- ironman often, but not always
- battlefield-limitation/battle-realism mode
- Karl Franz is the most important initial faction
- Immortal Empires is presumed but must be confirmed in the frozen baseline
- late-game challenge is the highest-value experience defect
