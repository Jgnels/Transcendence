# Project Charter

## Product definition

Transcendence is initially a personal enhancement project rather than a Workshop release.

It may improve campaign strategic AI, operational army coordination, battle behavior where technically controllable, late-game rival-power formation, diplomacy, empire management, pacing, observability, and explanation.

It is a vanilla-compatible core. SFO support and the owner's eventual full-stack configuration are optional profiles. The project is not limited to “AI” when another bounded, explicit, and fair game-system change better serves the desired experience.

## North star

Create campaigns in which the owner can become powerful without the world becoming inert.

The player should still face coherent rival states, meaningful fronts, dangerous offensives, hard battlefield choices, and plausible opponent recovery. Difficulty should come primarily from coordination, strategic pressure, asymmetric faction strengths, and well-formed threats—not arbitrary omniscience or invisible stat inflation.

## Initial optimization target

- Total War: WARHAMMER III, Immortal Empires;
- single player;
- hardest campaign and battle settings;
- battle realism;
- Karl Franz as the first priority faction;
- pure vanilla as the core validation environment;
- SFO as the first optional compatibility profile;
- late-game challenge as the highest-value defect.
