# Active Hypotheses

1. Late-game challenge collapse is caused less by tactical battle AI than by fragmented factions, poor army concentration, weak target selection, diplomacy churn, failure to recover and insufficient escalation around dominant powers.
2. A Three-Kingdoms-inspired rival-power system can improve late game without a universal anti-player coalition.
3. SFO's broader unit caps, pacing and campaign changes may make strategic coordination improvements more valuable than further stat bonuses.
4. Shadow-mode operational recommendations can be evaluated before direct campaign intervention.
5. Multiple synthetic labs should model different fidelity/cost levels rather than one lab claiming complete game fidelity.
