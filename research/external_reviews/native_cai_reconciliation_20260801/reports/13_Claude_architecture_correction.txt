4. Correcting the "not exposed" claim

That statement was wrong — it collapsed two different questions into one. Corrected:

OFFICIAL_SCRIPT_API_DOCUMENTED — the Lua campaign scripting surface. Established by CA's own modding docs and used by every mod I found. Doesn't need v0.2I.
CURRENT_SCHEMA_EXPOSED — whatever rpfm-schemas currently defines for 8.1.x tables. Fully offline.
DB_TUNABLE — whether a given table is a flat, moddable DB table vs. compiled logic. Determinable from the schema and RPFM decoder alone, offline.
PACK_PRIOR_ART_PROVEN — behaviors other mods demonstrably achieve live right now (e.g., AI Camping Settlements Fix changing idle-turn allowance). This is proof by someone else's running game, not yours — doesn't need your v0.2I.
DOCUMENTED_BUT_RUNTIME_UNVERIFIED — something the API/schema claims exists that your specific harness hasn't confirmed it can read/write in your environment. This is the one category that actually needs a live check.
ENGINE_INTERNAL_OR_UNEXPOSED — never touched by any documentation, schema, or mod. Only inferable, never provable, from outside CA.
UNKNOWN — not yet researched.

Most of "what does WH3 expose" is answerable in the first four categories, entirely offline. My original claim conflated capability discovery (mostly offline-answerable) with harness verification (the narrow slice that needs one live check). That was a real error, not a matter of framing.
