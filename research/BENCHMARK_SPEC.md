# Benchmark Specification — Draft v0

## Baselines

- Vanilla WH3 at the owner's exact settings.
- SFO-only at the owner's exact settings.
- Owner's full mod stack.
- Project shadow mode.
- Each enabled project intervention separately and combined.

## Campaign cohorts

- Karl Franz early survival and consolidation.
- Karl Franz midgame multi-front defense.
- Karl Franz late-game dominant empire.
- At least one non-human order faction.
- At least one destruction/chaos faction.
- At least one geographically isolated faction.
- Weak, equal and dominant player positions.

## Primary late-game metrics

- number and quality of coherent rival powers;
- credible enemy offensive capacity;
- coordinated-army operations;
- exposed-front pressure;
- strategic target quality;
- siege completion and reinforcement;
- opponent recovery after losses;
- war/diplomacy churn;
- idle armies and wasted travel;
- anti-player bias indicators;
- turn time and script cost;
- owner-rated tension, fairness, variety and tedium.

## Battle metrics

- value-adjusted casualties;
- idle unit time;
- target suitability;
- formation and flank integrity;
- reserve timing;
- ranged/artillery/spell value;
- capture and pursuit behavior;
- command latency;
- owner-rated tactical challenge and fairness.

## Rule

No benchmark is promoted to canonical until scenario setup, inputs, versions, mods and result collection are reproducible.
